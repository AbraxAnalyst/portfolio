About

Meet Abiodun, a Nigerian who experienced energy poverty firsthand and now leads change as Founder of Cosolar, driven by the vision that energy is the essence of life.

Hello there

Hi, I am leading the creation of Africa’s largest renewable-energy data structure, turning raw data into powerful insights for a sustainable future.

I am a results-driven Data Analyst and Renewable Energy Engineer with proven expertise in energy operations, smart metering, solar system design, and data-driven decision-making. My work sits at the intersection of analytics, technology, and clean energy, where I help businesses optimize performance, reduce cost, and enhance sustainability.

Over the years, I have installed and configured advanced smart energy meters, designed reliable off-grid and hybrid solar systems, built analytical dashboards for intelligent decision-making, and led teams across community development, technical operations, and project execution. My career is marked by excellence, including winning the IAENG 2025 Story Contest Award and deploying energy systems totaling over 400 KVA and 1 MWh in 2025. I am deeply passionate about solving Africa’s energy challenges through data, innovation, and strategic leadership, consistently delivering solutions that create real impact and measurable transformation.
